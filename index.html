WHO WE ARE

Hope grows when communities stand together.

Seeds of Hope Baringo is a community initiative formed to bring people together in practical support of households facing hardship.

We believe that compassion becomes meaningful when it leads to action. We are starting locally, listening to community needs and building relationships with individuals, volunteers, organisations and responsible partners who want to make a difference.

Our mission: To restore hope, strengthen community resilience and support vulnerable people through compassion, collective action and sustainable development.

OUR PRIORITIES

Where support can make a difference

Our initial focus is on urgent needs and practical community support in Baringo County.

01

Drought relief

Mobilising food and essential supplies for households affected by drought and food insecurity.

02

Vulnerable children

Supporting children facing hardship by connecting them with essential items and community support.

03

Struggling families

Working with caring supporters to respond to basic household needs and strengthen family wellbeing.

OUR COMMITMENT

Act with care. Work with communities. Be accountable.

We aim to listen first, identify needs with community input and coordinate support responsibly.

  • Put dignity and respect first.
  • Work transparently with supporters and community members.
  • Share verified updates about activities and needs.
  • Build partnerships that strengthen local solutions.

We will publish project updates and impact figures only when they have been verified.

OUR VISION

A resilient community where no one is left behind.

We want to help nurture a future in which families can face difficult seasons with greater support, opportunity and hope.
